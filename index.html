BEFORE WE BEGIN

Straight answers.

What does the R500 website assessment include?
A focused assessment of one agreed website scope, including common risk checks and a written report. Exact checks and deliverables are confirmed before testing.
What gets tested?
Checks are selected for the approved scope and may include common web risks, security headers, SSL/TLS configuration, software exposure and DNS where applicable.
Can testing affect my website?
No testing is entirely risk-free. Checks are scoped to avoid disruption; denial-of-service tests, destructive actions and production-data changes are excluded.
Do I need to provide permission?
Yes. Written authorisation from the owner or an authorised representative is required before any assessment begins.
What will I receive?
A confidential written report with findings, risk context and prioritised recommendations to help address issues.
Does the price include fixing issues?
No. Remediation and code changes are quoted separately unless they are explicitly included in a written agreement.
When does testing and payment happen?
The scope, price and payment terms are agreed before testing starts. The report is delivered after the agreed assessment is complete.
